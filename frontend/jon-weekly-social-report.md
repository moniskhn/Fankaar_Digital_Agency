## Quality Gates (Before Anything Goes Live)

### Missandei → Content Creation
- Writes captions, designs graphics, edits Reels
- Submits to **Approval Dashboard**

### Jon → Quality Review
- Reviews every Reel and major creative asset
- Checks brand voice, visual quality, accuracy
- Approves or rejects with feedback
- **Threshold:** Jon only reviews Reels and carousels. Single-image posts and text posts run auto-approved after Missandei creates them.

### Monis → Final Approval (Optional)
- Jon sends weekly report
- Monis can override or request changes
- If Monis is busy, Jon's approval stands

---

## Automation Rules

**Auto-publish (no review needed):**
- Text-only LinkedIn posts
- Instagram stories
- Twitter threads
- Single-image posts with standard templates

**Requires Jon review:**
- Instagram Reels
- Video content
- Carousels with 3+ slides
- Any post mentioning a client by name
- Paid ad creative

**Requires Monis approval:**
- Posts about pricing or packages
- Any crisis or PR response
- New content formats we haven't tested

---

## Weekly Report Delivery

**Time:** Every Monday, 8:00 AM Dubai time
**Format:** WhatsApp message (summary) + Dashboard deep-dive link
**Recipients:** Monis (primary), Sandor (if client inquiries are low)

**If metrics are below target:**
Jon flags it immediately: "Weekly metrics are 20% below target. Recommendations: [X]. Need your decision on [Y]."
