## Script 1: Cold Outreach (DM / LinkedIn / Email)

**Goal:** Get a 15-minute discovery call.

**Opening (Hook):**
> Hi [Name], I came across [Company] while researching [industry] businesses in Dubai. Your [specific thing — e.g., portfolio / recent project / reputation] caught my attention.
>
> Quick question: Are you currently handling your social media and digital marketing in-house, or do you have an agency partner?

**If they say "in-house" or "no agency":**
> That's what most [industry] businesses do at your stage. Here's the thing — we built something different. Fankaar Digital runs on 23 AI agents that specialize in different marketing disciplines. One agent handles your social, another your SEO, another your ads. They work 24/7 and remember everything about your brand.
>
> We're currently helping [similar company] grow their Instagram from zero to [number] in [timeframe]. I'd love to show you how we could do the same for [Company].
>
> Would a 15-minute call this week work? No pitch, just a look at what we'd build for you.

**If they say "we have an agency":**
> Got it. Most of our clients came to us from agencies they felt were too slow or too expensive. We use AI agents that create content in hours, not weeks. One client told us we delivered in a day what their old agency took a week to produce.
>
> Even if you're happy with your current setup, would you be open to a quick comparison? We can audit your current social presence and show you gaps — free, no obligation.

## Script 2: Discovery Call (15 minutes)

**Goal:** Understand needs and position the right package.

**Opening (2 min):**
> Thanks for making time. Before I say anything about us, I want to understand what you're actually trying to solve. What's the one thing about your current marketing that frustrates you most?

**Probe (5 min):**
- "How are you handling social media right now?"
- "What platforms matter most to you?"
- "Do you have existing content, or are we starting from scratch?"
- "What's your monthly marketing budget range?"
- "What does success look like in 3 months?"
- "Who's your main competitor doing this well?"

**Position (5 min):**
> Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd recommend:
>
> **Option A — Starter (AED 2,500/month):** Social media management + basic content. Perfect if you just need consistent posting and community management.
>
> **Option B — Growth (AED 5,000/month):** Everything in Starter + SEO + paid ads + monthly reporting. This is what most of our [industry] clients choose.
>
> **Option C — Premium (AED 8,000/month):** Full service — all 23 agents activated, including creative design, Reels, web development, competitive intelligence, and crisis management.

**Close (3 min):**
> Which of these feels closest to what you need? If none fit perfectly, we can customize. The next step is simple: I'll send you an onboarding form to collect your brand details, social handles, and goals. Once that's in, our agents start building your content calendar within 48 hours.
>
> Shall I send the form now?

## Script 4: Objection Handling

**"It's too expensive"**
> I hear that. Let's look at it differently: what's your current cost for marketing? If you're doing it in-house, factor in the time you're spending. If you have an agency, compare deliverables — we produce content in hours, not weeks. Most clients see the value within the first month when their engagement jumps.
>
> What if we started with the Starter package for one month? If you don't see clear value, we part ways. No lock-in.

**"I need to think about it"**
> Of course. While you're thinking, would it help if I sent you a free audit of your current social media presence? Takes me 5 minutes to run, and you'll see exactly where the gaps are. That might make the decision clearer.

**"I've been burned by agencies before"**
> I get it. That's actually why most of our clients chose us — they were tired of slow agencies, junior staff, and inconsistent quality. Our agents don't have bad days, don't quit, and don't forget your brand voice. Everything is transparent: you see every post before it goes live, you approve every Reel, you control the calendar.
>
> Want to try us for 30 days risk-free? If the work isn't good, you don't pay month two.

**"I don't have time to review content"**
> That's the beauty of our system. You don't review every post — you set the brand voice once, and our agents execute. You only review Reels and major creative assets. Everything else runs on autopilot. Most clients spend 15 minutes per week in the approval dashboard.

**"Can I see examples of your work?"**
> Absolutely. Here's our portfolio page: [Link]. You'll see Reels, posters, and content calendars we've built for clients in [their industry]. Want me to also send a sample content calendar tailored to your business? I can have that ready in an hour.

## Script 5: Closing the Deal

**Goal:** Get the signed agreement and first payment.

> [Name], we've talked about your goals, I've shown you the package, and you've seen our work. Here's what happens when you say yes:
>
> 1. You fill out the onboarding form (10 minutes)
> 2. We send you the service agreement to e-sign
> 3. You pay the first month's fee + setup fee (AED [amount])
> 4. Within 48 hours, your content calendar is ready
> 5. Within one week, your first posts go live
>
> What's the one thing holding you back from starting?

**If they hesitate:**
> Let's make this easy. I'll send you the agreement and onboarding form right now. You can review both over the weekend. No pressure — but every week you wait is a week your competitors are building their presence and you're not.
>
> Fair?

## Script 6: Upsell (Existing Client)

**Goal:** Move client to higher package or add services.

> Hi [Name], I've been reviewing your campaign performance. Your social media is doing well — engagement is up [X]% and followers grew by [Y] in the last month.
>
> I noticed something: your website traffic from social is strong, but your conversion rate is low. That tells me people are interested, but something's not closing the deal.
>
> I'd recommend adding our Landing Page + CRO service (Gendry's team). We can build a high-conversion landing page specifically for your social traffic. One client saw a 40% increase in inquiries after we did this.
>
> It adds AED 2,000/month to your package. Want me to have Gendry build a mockup?

## Script 7: Re-Engagement (Lost Lead)

**Goal:** Bring back a lead that went cold.

> Hi [Name], it's been a few weeks since we spoke. I wanted to check in — no sales pitch.
>
> I was thinking about your business and wondered: did you end up solving the [problem they mentioned]? If so, great. If not, I'd love to understand what changed.
>
> Either way, I respect your time and won't keep following up if you're not interested. Just let me know.
