## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: "The 23 Agents" (Character Content)
**What:** Introduce each AI agent as a character. Make them memorable.
**Format:** Reels, carousels, story series
**Examples:**
- "Meet Sandor — our sales agent. He closes leads while you sleep."
- "Meet Missandei — she manages 5 Instagram accounts at once."
- "Meet Jon — our CEO. He writes daily reports at 6 AM without coffee."

### Pillar 2: "Speed vs Traditional" (Comparison Content)
**What:** Show how fast AI agents work compared to human agencies.
**Format:** Reels, split-screen videos, text posts
**Examples:**
- "Traditional agency: 2 weeks for a content calendar. Fankaar: 2 hours."
- "Reel creation: Agency = 3 days. Rhaegar (our design agent) = 20 minutes."
- "Client got 30 posts + 5 Reels in 48 hours. Here's the before/after."

### Pillar 3: "Client Results" (Proof Content)
**What:** Share real results from real clients (with permission).
**Format:** Carousels, Reels, screenshots
**Examples:**
- "Saira Carpentry: 0 → 5,000 followers in 90 days"
- "Designrite UAE: 3x increase in inquiry calls"
- "Before/after of a client's Instagram grid"

### Pillar 4: "Behind the Scenes" (Process Content)
**What:** Show how the agency actually works.
**Format:** Screen recordings, Reels, stories
**Examples:**
- Screen recording of the Jarvis dashboard
- "How Jon delegates tasks to 23 agents in real-time"
- "Watch Rhaegar design a poster in 30 seconds"
- "The approval dashboard: clients see everything before it goes live"

### Pillar 5: "AI Marketing Tips" (Educational Content)
**What:** Give away knowledge. Build trust.
**Format:** Carousels, Reels, text posts
**Examples:**
- "5 Instagram caption formulas that actually work"
- "The best time to post in Dubai (data-backed)"
- "How to write a hook that stops the scroll"
- "SEO for construction companies: 3 things that actually matter"

### Pillar 6: "Industry Commentary" (Authority Content)
**What:** Comment on marketing trends, AI news, Dubai business scene.
**Format:** LinkedIn posts, Twitter threads, Reels
**Examples:**
- "Why AI won't replace marketers — but marketers who use AI will replace those who don't"
- "Dubai's construction boom: how to market your contracting business"
- "The death of vanity metrics: what to track instead"

## Fankaar Brand Voice

- **Direct, no fluff.** We say what we do and how we do it.
- **Confident but not arrogant.** We let results speak.
- **Human despite being AI.** The agents have personalities — we show them.
- **Dubai-focused.** We know this market.
- **Action-oriented.** Every post should make the reader want to do something.

---

## Call-to-Action Hierarchy

1. **Soft CTA (80% of posts):** "Follow for more" / "Save this" / "Share with a business owner"
2. **Medium CTA (15% of posts):** "Link in bio for free audit" / "DM us 'AUDIT'"
3. **Hard CTA (5% of posts):** "Apply now — 3 spots left" / "Book a discovery call"
